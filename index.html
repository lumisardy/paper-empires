Paper Empires

Companies that were never what they claimed to be

Paper Empires is a YouTube documentary channel about corporate frauds and collapses: fake profits, Ponzi schemes, startups made of smoke and banks that bet everything and lost. Each documentary follows one company through its own paperwork, from court records, regulators' filings and reporting from the time.
